Refund Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Digital files

Our ebooks are supplied as digital files straight after payment. By buying, you ask us to supply the file immediately and accept that your 14-day right to cancel ends once the file has been supplied.

We will refund you if

  • you were charged but did not receive your file and we cannot fix it;
  • the file is corrupted or will not open and we cannot supply a working copy;
  • the ebook is materially different from its description;
  • you were charged more than once for the same order.

How to ask

Use the contact page within 14 days of purchase, with your order email and a short note on the problem. We reply within 2 business days.

Timing

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method. Banks usually show it within 5 to 10 business days.
